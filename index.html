CURRICULUM

Four layers, one build.

3D Printing

Students design and print their own chassis parts and drone components — CAD screen to physical part, same class.

Drone Technology

Build and fly. Flight principles, stabilisation, and control, learned by wiring and flight-testing real frames.

Applied AI

Age-appropriate introductions to how machines sense, decide, and act — built into robots students have assembled themselves.

RoboPreneurship

A dedicated track where students pitch, cost, and present their builds — thinking like founders, not just makers.

Classes run without individual laptop access by design — time goes into building and wiring, not screen time.

PROGRAMS

Grade bands

Hands-on kits, project workbooks and LMS access — structured by grade band, from first circuits to a RoboPreneurship capstone.

Grades
1–2

Tinker Explorers

Play-based discovery with simple circuits, magnets & batteries, safe snap-together builds, and unplugged games that build sequencing & logic before screens.

Grades
3–5

Foundation Builders

Block-based coding (Scratch), first LED & sensor circuits with Arduino, simple machines, and an introduction to 3D shapes & printing.

Grades
6–9

Junior Innovator

AI literacy through Scratch (how AI sees, hears & understands), hands-on electronics (transistors, sensors, motors), CAD design, first 3D prints & drone-building basics.

Grades
10–12

RoboPreneurship Track

Advanced electronics & embedded systems, Python & AI projects, FPV drone piloting, product design with 3D printing, and a RoboPreneurship pitch capstone.

OUR STORY

Why GNR Robotics exists.

After 6+ years building strong academic outcomes across preschool, K-12 tuition and IIT-foundation coaching, SRS EduTech kept seeing the same gap: students doing well on paper, with little hands-on exposure to the technology shaping their future.

GNR Robotics was built to close that gap — not with another block-coding kit, but with a program that puts 3D printers, drone parts, and real entrepreneurship thinking in front of students who are ready for more than a manual.
